# Kartway Returns and Refunds Policy

**KARTWAY OPERATIONS MANUAL**  
**SECTION 2: RETURNS AND REFUNDS POLICY**  
**EFFECTIVE DATE: 15 JANUARY 2024**  
**DOCUMENT ID: KW-POL-SR-042**  

---

### 2.0 Standard Return Windows

2.1 The standard return window for merchandise purchased on Kartway is thirty (30) calendar days from the verified date of delivery, unless an exception is explicitly defined in clauses 2.2 through 2.5 of this policy.

2.2 Electronics and motorized devices, including smartphones, laptops, audio equipment, and smart home appliances, are subject to a strict fifteen (15) calendar day return window from the date of delivery.

2.3 Apparel, footwear, and fashion accessories qualify for an extended return window of forty-five (45) calendar days from the date of delivery, provided items adhere to the condition standards set out in clause 2.6.

2.4 Consumable groceries, fresh produce, opened personal care items, and custom-manufactured goods are classified as non-returnable and are ineligible for return or refund under any circumstance, except where items arrive spoiled, damaged, or expired as governed by clause 2.11.

2.5 Third-party marketplace items fulfilled directly by independent merchants operating on Kartway may carry distinct return windows as specified on the individual product listing page, provided such windows are not shorter than fourteen (14) calendar days.

### 2.6 Item Condition Requirements

2.7 To qualify for any refund under this policy, returned merchandise must satisfy the following baseline condition criteria:
* (a) The item must be complete with all original manufacturer packaging, tags, seals, instruction manuals, and accessories.
* (b) Electronics must be factory-reset, removed from all user accounts (including iCloud and Google accounts), and free of any user-generated passwords or locks.
* (c) Apparel must be unworn, unwashed, free of any odors or cosmetic stains, and retain all original hanging tags.

2.8 Items returned in a condition that violates clause 2.7 will be rejected by the Kartway Returns Processing Center located at 400 Logistics Parkway, Building 7, and returned to the customer at the customer's shipping expense.

### 2.9 Return Shipping Fee Allocation

2.10 Kartway shall bear the full cost of return shipping in scenarios where the return is initiated due to Kartway or merchant error. Qualifying error scenarios are strictly limited to:
* (a) Receipt of a defective, broken, or non-functioning item.
* (b) Receipt of an incorrect item (wrong size, color, or model relative to the confirmed order).
* (c) Items damaged in transit by a Kartway-contracted logistics carrier.

2.11 In scenarios where the customer initiates a return due to personal preference, change of mind, incorrect sizing ordered by the customer, or subjective dissatisfaction, the customer shall pay a flat return shipping fee of $5.99, which shall be automatically deducted from the final refund amount issued.

### 2.12 Refund Processing Timelines

2.13 Approved refunds shall be initiated by the Kartway Finance Department within two (2) business days following the physical inspection and acceptance of the returned item at the processing facility.

2.14 Funds shall be credited to the customer based on the original payment method utilized at checkout, subject to the following processing timelines:
* (a) Credit card, debit card, and direct bank transfer refunds require five (5) to seven (7) business days to post to the customer's financial institution account.
* (b) Kartway Digital Wallet balance refunds are processed and available within twenty-four (24) hours of approval.
* (c) Cash on Delivery (COD) order refunds require ten (10) business days to process via electronic bank transfer, pending the customer's submission of verified banking details through the Kartway customer portal.

### 2.15 Partial-Refund Schedule for Opened Items

2.16 Non-electronic merchandise that has been opened, unsealed, or subject to light handling—while remaining fully functional and complete—shall be subjected to a mandatory restocking fee and partial refund schedule as follows:
* (a) Items returned with broken manufacturer seals but showing zero signs of physical use shall receive a refund equal to eighty-five percent (85%) of the purchase price (15% restocking fee).
* (b) Items showing minor cosmetic handling, missing non-essential secondary packaging (such as outer cardboard sleeves), or missing promotional accessories shall receive a refund equal to seventy percent (70%) of the purchase price (30% restocking fee).
* (c) Items missing primary internal packaging, manuals, or essential functional accessories shall receive a refund equal to fifty percent (50%) of the purchase price (50% restocking fee).

2.17 Opened electronics are exempt from the partial-refund schedule outlined in clause 2.16 and shall be assessed a flat twenty percent (20%) restocking fee, provided the device has logged fewer than two (2) hours of operational uptime as verified by diagnostic software at the processing center. Devices exceeding two (2) hours of operational uptime shall be rejected entirely under clause 2.8.
