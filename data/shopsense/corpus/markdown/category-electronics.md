# Category Policy Addendum: Electronics

**KARTWAY OPERATIONS MANUAL**
**ADDENDUM KW-POL-204: ELECTRONICS CATEGORY POLICY**
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025
**Applies To:** Customer Support Tier 1, Tier 2, and Resolution Specialists
**Supersedes:** KW-POL-109 (Legacy Electronics Addendum)

---

### 1. SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS

1.1. This document establishes mandatory procedures for customer operations agents handling returns, exchanges, warranties, and hazardous materials claims within the Electronics category on the Kartway marketplace. 

1.2. The rules set forth in this addendum supersede standard marketplace policies where specific numerical thresholds or processing rules differ.

---

### 2. RETURN WINDOW

2.1. The standard return window for all items listed under the Electronics category is strictly **15 calendar days** from the verified delivery date recorded by the carrier.

2.2. Return requests initiated on day 16 or later shall be automatically rejected by the automated support portal, subject only to the exceptions detailed in Section 7 of this policy.

---

### 3. CONDITION REQUIREMENTS AND PACKAGING

3.1. To qualify for a full refund, returned electronic items must meet the following condition criteria:
*   The device must be free of physical damage, liquid ingress, and unauthorized modifications.
*   All pre-installed manufacturer protective films must remain intact unless the item is an open-box listing.
*   All factory-issued accessories—including charging bricks, data cables, remote controls, and detachable stands—must be included in the return parcel.

3.2. **Original Packaging Mandate:** Original manufacturer packaging (retail box) is **mandatory** for all electronics returns. The return must include the inner cardboard bracing, styrofoam inserts, and outer carton matching the item's serial number.

3.3. If an item is returned missing its original packaging or with damaged packaging, agents shall apply a mandatory **15% packaging devaluation fee** against the refund total.

---

### 4. RESTOCKING FEES

4.1. A standard restocking fee of **8.5%** shall be deducted from the customer's refund for all opened, non-defective electronic returns processed through the Kartway return portal.

4.2. Restocking fees are waived only if:
*   The item arrived in a defective or non-working state.
*   The customer received an incorrect item due to a seller fulfillment error.
*   The return is processed under the direct supervision of Senior Resolution Specialist Marcus Vance.

---

### 5. HAZARDOUS MATERIALS AND LITHIUM-ION HANDLING

5.1. Electronics containing lithium-ion batteries, secondary cells, or internal chemical energy storage are classified as Kartway Hazmat Class E-3.

5.2. Customers returning items containing lithium-ion batteries must adhere to the following safety protocols:
*   The device must be powered completely off prior to boxing; sleep or standby modes are prohibited.
*   Exposed terminals must be insulated with non-conductive tape.
*   The exterior return shipping carton must display a completed Kartway Hazmat E-3 lithium battery handling label, obtainable via the customer return portal.

5.3. Agents are strictly prohibited from generating prepaid return labels for swollen, punctured, or leaking lithium batteries. Such items must be handled via the Kartway Direct Disposal Protocol managed by the environmental compliance team, and the customer shall be issued an immediate full refund without requiring physical transit.

---

### 6. WARRANTY PERIODS

6.1. The standard marketplace limited warranty for new electronics purchased on Kartway is **12 months** from the date of purchase, unless the manufacturer’s commercial warranty specifies a longer duration.

6.2. Certified Refurbished electronics carry a statutory warranty period of **90 calendar days** from the date of purchase.

6.3. Warranty claims submitted after the expiration of the windows defined in 6.1 and 6.2 must be redirected directly to the authorized brand service center; support agents shall not issue return merchandise authorizations (RMAs) for out-of-warranty claims.

---

### 7. NAMED EXCEPTIONS

7.1. **Enterprise Computing Hardware:** Enterprise-grade servers, rack-mounted storage arrays, and commercial networking switches listed under sub-category 44-NC are subject to a strict **7-day** return window and carry a non-negotiable **20%** restocking fee upon return, regardless of condition.

7.2. **Audio-Visual Custom Installations:** Projection equipment and integrated home theater audio receivers valued above $2,500.00 USD are final sale upon opening and are exempt from the standard 15-day return window, eligible for warranty repair only.
