# Category Policy Addendum: Beauty

# KARTWAY OPERATIONS POLICY MANUAL
## ADDENDUM 44-B: BEAUTY CATEGORY PROTOCOLS
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2026  
**Supersedes:** Version 4.1  
**Applicability:** All Tier-1 and Tier-2 Support Agents handling Beauty, Personal Care, and Cosmetics verticals.

---

### 44.1 Return Window
1.1 Notwithstanding standard Marketplace Operating Procedure 12.1 (which mandates a 30-day return window), all items classified under the Beauty category are subject to a strict **14-calendar-day** return window, calculated from the verified delivery timestamp provided by the carrier.  
1.2 Return authorization requests initiated on day 15 or later shall be automatically rejected by the ticketing system without agent discretion.

### 44.2 Category-Specific Condition Requirements
2.1 Used, tested, or swatched cosmetics, skincare, and hair care products are strictly non-returnable due to hygiene and health regulations.  
2.2 To qualify for a return under Section 44.1, items must be unopened, factory-sealed, and retain all tamper-evident seals, shrink-wrap, and safety bands intact.  
2.3 Any item received with broken seals, missing safety caps, or evidence of puncture shall be classified as "Biohazard Contaminated Return" and disposed of immediately without issuing a refund.

### 44.3 Original Packaging Mandate
3.1 Original manufacturer packaging is strictly mandatory for all Beauty category returns.  
3.2 Returns received without the original outer carton, or where the outer carton is heavily defaced with tape or shipping labels applied directly to the product box, shall incur a mandatory packaging rejection.  
3.3 Plain brown replacement boxes used by customers to ship items back are acceptable only if the pristine original manufacturer packaging remains enclosed inside.

### 44.4 Restocking Fees
4.1 A mandatory restocking fee of **17.5%** shall be deducted from the merchandise refund amount on all approved non-defective Beauty category returns to cover secondary inspection and quarantine processing.  
4.2 The restocking fee is waived exclusively if the return is the direct result of a Kartway logistics fulfillment error (e.g., wrong item shipped).

### 44.5 Hazardous and Perishable Handling Rules
5.1 Certain beauty items containing pressurized aerosols, ethanol-based perfumes, or volatile organic compounds are classified as hazardous materials (Hazmat) and cannot be returned via standard postal drop-offs.  
5.2 Customers attempting to return Hazmat-designated beauty products must be issued the specialized Kartway Hazardous Return Label, routing the package exclusively through certified ground carrier Hazmat channels.  
5.3 Perishable organic beauty items lacking synthetic preservatives must bear a manufacture date no older than **180 days** at the time of initial warehouse intake; any customer return of a perishable item past this age threshold must be quarantined and marked as expired stock.

### 44.6 Warranty Period
6.1 Electrical beauty appliances (including hair dryers, straighteners, facial cleansing brushes, and IPL devices) carry a manufacturer-backed warranty period of **730 days** (2 years) from the original purchase date.  
6.2 Consumable liquids, creams, powders, and disposable applicator tools carry no operational warranty once opened or past the 14-day return window outlined in Section 44.1.
