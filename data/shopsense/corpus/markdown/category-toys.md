# Category Policy Addendum: Toys

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY ADDENDUM
## SECTION 4: TOYS CATEGORY (T-400)
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025  
**Applies To:** All Tier-1 and Tier-2 Support Agents, Dispute Resolution Specialists, and Return Processing Hubs.  
**Supersedes:** General Marketplace Policy Section 1.2 (Standard Returns) and Section 3.1 (Packaging Standards) for all items listed under the Toys and Hobbies root node.

---

### 4.1 Return Window and Deadlines
1. **Standard Window Exception:** Notwithstanding the 30-day marketplace standard, all items within the Toys category are subject to an extended return window of **45 calendar days** from the verified delivery date, to accommodate gift-giving cycles.
2. **Holiday Extension:** For orders fulfilled between 15 November and 24 December, the return window is extended until **31 January** of the following year.
3. **Return Initiation Cutoff:** Return requests must be initiated via the Kartway Buyer Portal no later than 23:59 local time on the 45th day following delivery. Support agents are strictly prohibited from manually overriding expired return windows beyond the 45-day threshold without written authorization from Category Lead Marcus Vance.

### 4.2 Condition Requirements and Open-Box Rules
1. **General Condition:** Returned toys must be in either factory-sealed condition or complete open-box condition. 
2. **Missing Components:** Items missing core functional components, remote controls, assembly hardware, or instruction manuals are ineligible for full refund. Agents may authorize a partial refund in accordance with Section 4.5.
3. **Safety and Hygiene:** Items intended for oral contact (e.g., teething toys, whistles, baby rattles) or water immersion are strictly non-returnable if the sterile inner seal is broken. Agents must reject return requests for these sub-categories immediately upon customer disclosure of seal tampering.

### 4.3 Original Packaging Mandate
1. **Mandatory Packaging:** Original manufacturer packaging (boxes, plastic blisters, display tins) is mandatory for all collectable figures, board games, and electronic learning toys.
2. **Damaged Packaging Threshold:** If the outer packaging is destroyed, defaced with courier labels, or missing, agents shall apply a mandatory repackaging deduction unless the item arrived damaged due to inadequate transit packaging by the third-party seller.
3. **Plain-Box Returns:** Returns received in plain brown shipping cartons without the original branded product box must be flagged as "Non-compliant Packaging" and assessed a processing penalty per Section 4.5.

### 4.4 Restocking Fees
1. **Standard Restocking Fee:** A restocking fee of **12%** of the item purchase price shall be deducted from the refund total for any open-box toy return where the customer changes their mind ("buyer's remorse").
2. **Waiver Conditions:** The restocking fee is waived if:
   * The item is factory-sealed and unopened.
   * The return reason is "Defective," "Not as Described," or "Damaged in Transit."
   * The seller is participating in the Kartway Prime Toy Logistics program.

### 4.5 Hazardous Materials, Perishables, and Chemical Safety
1. **Chemical and Chemical-Adjacent Toys:** Chemistry sets, crystal-growing kits, slime-making compounds, and projectile toys must be inspected for leakage or activation prior to restock.
2. **Perishable Novelties:** Novelty food-grade items or gel-filled sensory toys possessing an active expiration date must have a remaining shelf life of at least **180 days** at the time of return receipt to be eligible for restock. Items failing this threshold must be routed to hazardous destruction.
3. **Lithium Battery Compliance:** Toys containing internal lithium-ion batteries that show physical swelling, puncturing, or leakage must be handled pursuant to Kartway Hazmat Protocol H-9. Agents must issue an immediate full refund and instruct the buyer to dispose of the item locally via certified e-waste facilities; return transit of damaged batteries is strictly prohibited.

### 4.6 Warranty Period
1. **Manufacturer Warranty Coordination:** Kartway provides a limited operational warranty for electronic and motorized toys lasting **90 calendar days** from the date of purchase, covering mechanical and electrical failure not caused by user misuse, liquid ingress, or drops.
2. **Extended Claims:** For claims arising between day 91 and the expiration of the manufacturer’s own stated warranty, agents shall direct the customer to the brand's authorized service center and provide the original Kartway digital invoice. Agents are not authorized to issue replacement units under Kartway policy after the 90-day window closes.
