# Category Policy Addendum: Home & Kitchen

**KARTWAY OPERATIONS MANUAL**
**ADDENDUM 4.9: HOME & KITCHEN CATEGORY POLICY**
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2026
**Document Owner:** Sarah Vance, Customer Operations Policy Lead
**Applies To:** All Tier 1, Tier 2, and Specialist Support Agents

---

### 1.0 Purpose and Scope
This addendum establishes category-specific handling rules, return parameters, and warranty enforcement standards for all transactions within the Kartway Home & Kitchen marketplace vertical. Where this document conflicts with the General Operations Baseline, the rules herein supersede.

### 2.0 Return Window
2.1 The return window for all Home & Kitchen merchandise is exactly **30 calendar days** from the verified delivery date. 
2.2 This duration matches the general Kartway returns baseline; no category-specific extensions, seasonal waivers, or reductions apply.
2.3 Return requests initiated on day 31 or later must be systematically rejected by support agents without exception.

### 3.0 Condition Requirements
3.1 To qualify for a refund, returned Home & Kitchen items must meet the following baseline conditions:
*   **Cookware and Utensils:** Must show zero signs of thermal exposure, heat tinting, or food residue. Items washed in a dishwasher that exhibit water spotting or detergent etching are classified as used and are ineligible for return.
*   **Small Appliances (e.g., blenders, air fryers):** Motor run-time must not exceed **14 minutes**, verified via internal diagnostic logs where applicable. 
*   **Textiles (e.g., bedding, tablecloths):** Must be free of pet hair, human dander, laundering odors, and synthetic fabric softeners.

### 4.0 Original Packaging Mandate
4.1 Original manufacturer packaging (inner box, styrofoam inserts, and carton sleeves) is **mandatory** for all small appliances, glassware, and ceramic dinnerware valued above **$45.00 USD**.
4.2 If an item under this value threshold is returned without original packaging, agents shall deduct a flat **$7.50 USD** repacking and containment fee from the refund total.
4.3 Items valued above $45.00 USD returned without original packaging shall be rejected at the dock and returned to the seller at the buyer's expense, unless accompanied by an approved pre-authorization code issued by a Tier 2 escalation lead.

### 5.0 Restocking Fees
5.1 A standard restocking fee of **12%** of the item purchase price shall be applied to all opened large-format kitchen appliances (including espresso machines, stand mixers, and convection ovens).
5.2 Unopened items in factory-sealed cartons are exempt from restocking fees.
5.3 Defective or damaged-on-arrival (DOA) items are strictly exempt from all restocking fees under operational code HK-DEF-09.

### 6.0 Hazardous and Perishable Handling Rules
6.1 **Perishables:** Food-grade items, culinary oils, and consumable meal kits listed under Home & Kitchen are **non-returnable and non-refundable**, regardless of whether the seal is intact, due to health code regulations. Agents must issue an immediate store credit for verified shipping damage rather than processing a physical return.
6.2 **Hazardous Materials:** Items containing pressurized gas cylinders (e.g., whipped cream dispensers, soda carbonators) or chemical descalers classified under hazardous transport codes must not be returned via standard postal drop-offs. Agents must issue a prepaid hazardous-class return label via regional carrier *Apex Freight* and apply a mandatory **$15.00 USD** hazardous processing surcharge to the merchant account ledger.

### 7.1 Manufacturer and Marketplace Warranty Period
7.1 The standard Kartway marketplace operational warranty for non-electronic Home & Kitchen goods is **180 calendar days** from delivery.
7.2 Electrical small appliances carry a manufacturer-backed warranty of **365 calendar days**, during which Kartway support agents must direct customers to authorized service centers before issuing marketplace-funded replacements or refunds.
